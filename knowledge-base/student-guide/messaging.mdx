**Messages** on the web app is your direct line to your teacher between lessons.

![Messages inbox](/images/student-guide/15-messages.webp)

- Open a thread with your teacher to ask questions, share what you want to work on, or get homework and resources.
- **System notifications** (like *Lesson Scheduled*) arrive in the same inbox, so you always know when a lesson is booked.
- The unread badge in the sidebar shows when you have new messages.

Click **Start conversation** to message your teacher, or reply in an existing thread.

<Tip>
Before a lesson, send your teacher a quick note about what you'd like to focus on (a grammar category from [Grammar Insights](/knowledge-base/student-guide/grammar-insights), a situation you're nervous about, an upcoming interview). It makes the lesson far more useful.
</Tip>
