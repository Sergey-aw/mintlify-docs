**Grammar Insights** (web app, marked *Beta*) zooms out from a single lesson to show patterns across **all** your lessons. It has **Mistakes** and **Grammar** tabs.

## Mistakes

![Grammar Insights — mistakes overview](/images/student-guide/12-grammar-insights-mistakes.png)

- **Total Mistakes**, **Mistakes/Hour**, **Total Hours** of learning, and the number of mistake **Categories**.
- **Mistakes by Category** — your distribution across grammar categories (Articles, Tense, Verb Form, Word Order, Prepositions, Determiners…). Click any category to see the breakdown.

This tells you, at a glance, what to focus on — usually your biggest one or two categories.

## Grammar

![Grammar Insights — grammar mastery](/images/student-guide/13-grammar-insights-grammar.png)

- **Total / Unique / In Progress / Acquired** grammar structures.
- **Grammar CEFR Progress** — how much of each level (A1 → C2) you've mastered, with a **Browse** link to explore the structures at each level.

<Tip>
Use this before a lesson: tell your teacher which category you want to focus on (e.g. "articles" or "past tenses"), and bring it up in your AI practice too.
</Tip>
