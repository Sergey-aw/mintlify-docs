The AI practice app at [chat.justtalk.ai](https://chat.justtalk.ai) (and on iOS) is where you keep talking **between** lessons. You speak naturally with AI characters, and everything you say feeds the same progress profile your teacher sees.

## Your home screen

![AI app home](/images/student-guide/ai-10-home.png)

The home screen pulls together:

- **Personal Scenarios** — practice scenarios your teacher assigned to you (for example *Grammar Practice: Perfect Present Tense*, *HR Screening*).
- **Pronunciation** — start a baseline assessment to find sounds to improve.
- **Recommended for You** — words picked for your goals; tap one to add it to your Vocabulary Builder.
- **Learning Paths** and **Continue Learning** — multi-step journeys and recent scenarios.

## Characters & scenarios

You can practice with different **personalities** — some warm and supportive, some challenging — to get comfortable with real conversations.

![AI characters](/images/student-guide/ai-02-personalities.png)

And you choose the **situation**, from casual small talk to high-pressure moments.

![Practice scenarios](/images/student-guide/ai-03-scenarios.png)

## Role-plays

Open **Role-plays** for the full library, organized by category — Business, Daily Life, Dating, Education, Interview, Small Talk, Social, Travel — each with its own set of scenarios.

![Role-play categories](/images/student-guide/ai-20-roleplays.png)

## Learning Paths

Some topics are **multi-step journeys**. A path moves you through stages (for example, a dating series: *Initial Meeting → Calling to Schedule → Date → Post-Date Chat → Freetalk*), tracking which steps you've completed.

![Learning Paths](/images/student-guide/ai-11-learning-paths.png)

Browse everything under **Continue Learning**, where scenarios are tagged by category (Daily Life, Education, Interview…).

![Continue Learning](/images/student-guide/ai-12-continue-learning.png)
