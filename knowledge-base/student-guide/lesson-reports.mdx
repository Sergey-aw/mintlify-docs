After each lesson, open it from **Lessons → View**. The report has five tabs plus your Focus Set and an AI summary.

![Lesson overview with Focus Set and summary](/images/student-guide/03-lesson-overview.webp)

## Overview

Your **Focus Set** for the lesson and an **AI-generated summary** — the main topics, key corrections, new vocabulary, and suggested next steps. A quick way to remember what the lesson covered.

## Transcript

The full word-for-word transcript of the lesson, with speaker labels. You can replay individual lines and translate them.

## Mistakes

Your mistakes grouped by category (Articles, Tense, Verb Form, Word Order, Prepositions…) with percentages, plus an **All Mistakes** list showing what you said, the correction, and a short explanation.

![Lesson mistakes by category](/images/student-guide/04-lesson-mistakes.webp)

## Vocabulary

Every meaningful word you used, with **Total / Unique / New / Acquired** counts and progress bars for common-word bands (100, 200, 500 most common…). Green badges show how many words you newly **acquired** in that lesson.

![Lesson vocabulary with frequency bands](/images/student-guide/05-lesson-vocabulary.webp)

## Grammar

The grammar structures detected in your speech, shown by CEFR level (A1 → C2), with counts and examples — so you can see whether you're stretching into higher-level structures.

![Lesson grammar by CEFR level](/images/student-guide/06-lesson-grammar.webp)

![Lesson grammar by CEFR level](/images/student-guide/06-lesson-grammar-1.webp)

![Lesson grammar by CEFR level](/images/student-guide/06-lesson-grammar-2.webp)

<Tip>
Turn your report into homework: pick 2–3 mistakes to avoid next time, and add a few **new** words from the Vocabulary tab to your [Focus Set](/knowledge-base/student-guide/vocabulary-builder) so they start counting toward your progress.
</Tip>
