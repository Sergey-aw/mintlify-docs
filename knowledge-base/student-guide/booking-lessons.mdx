Live lessons happen on the web app at [app.justtalk.ai](https://app.justtalk.ai).

## Book a lesson

Click **Book a Lesson** on your dashboard. Pick your teacher, then choose an open slot in their weekly availability.

![Book a lesson — pick teacher and time slot](/images/student-guide/16-book-lesson.png)

- Each teacher card shows their **rate**, lessons completed, and your **credit** balance.
- The time grid is color-coded: **Available**, **Your Lesson**, **Teacher Busy**, **Unavailable**.
- A lesson is **50 minutes** and costs **1 credit**.
- If you're out of credits, use **Buy lessons** on the teacher's card to purchase a package.

<Note>
Your teacher can also schedule a lesson for you directly — it will appear in your calendar and you'll get a notification in **Messages**.
</Note>

## Your calendar

Open **Calendar** to see your booked lessons in **Week** or **Month** view. Navigate with the arrows or jump to **Today**.

![Student calendar](/images/student-guide/14-calendar.png)

## Your lessons list

**Lessons** shows every past and upcoming session. Filter with the **All / Tutor / AI** tabs, by date range, or by status. Both your **teacher lessons** (50 min) and your **AI Tutor practice** (30 min) appear here together.

![Lessons list](/images/student-guide/02-lessons-list.png)

## Join a live lesson

When it's time, open the lesson and click **Join lesson**. The video room is built in — no Zoom or Google Meet needed. During the lesson your teacher can see your **Focus Set** (5 words to use), and JustTalk records the session so it can build your report afterward.

![Lesson page with Join lesson and Focus Set](/images/student-guide/03-lesson-overview.png)

<Tip>
Speak in full sentences and use your Focus Set words. The more you say, the richer your vocabulary, grammar, and pronunciation feedback will be.
</Tip>

After the lesson, head to your [lesson report](/knowledge-base/student-guide/lesson-reports) to see what you used and what to work on.
