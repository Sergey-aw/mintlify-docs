## Having a conversation

Tap any scenario to start. You talk with the AI character by voice — press the mic and speak naturally; you can interrupt, ask questions, or end any time.

![Live AI conversation](/images/student-guide/ai-13-conversation.png)

Helpful tools during a chat:

- **Mic button** — hold/press to speak.
- **Help me answer** — get a suggestion if you're stuck.
- **Audio replay** and **translate** icons on each AI message.
- For a Learning Path, the **step navigator** at the bottom shows where you are in the journey.

![Conversation with learning-path steps](/images/student-guide/ai-18-conversation-history.png)

## Conversation Insights

When you finish, tap **Feedback** to open your **Conversation Insights** — instant, detailed coaching on what you just said.

![Conversation Insights — overall score](/images/student-guide/ai-14-conversation-insights.png)

It includes:

- **Overall Score** out of 3, with a **Score Breakdown** per criterion — **Clarity**, **Range**, **Flow** — each rated and explained, with real quotes from your speech.

  ![Insights — criteria with examples](/images/student-guide/ai-15-insights-criteria.png)

- **Mistakes to Fix** — each tagged by category (e.g. `word_order`, `vocabulary`) showing **what you said** vs a **better** version, with a short explanation.

  ![Insights — mistakes to fix](/images/student-guide/ai-16-insights-mistakes.png)

- **Patterns in Your Speech**, your **Vocabulary Level**, and **Fluency Observations** — the bigger-picture habits to work on.

  ![Insights — patterns and level](/images/student-guide/ai-17-insights-patterns.png)

<Tip>
The vocabulary, grammar, and pronunciation from these AI chats roll up into the **same** progress your teacher sees — so practicing here directly improves the numbers on your dashboard.
</Tip>
