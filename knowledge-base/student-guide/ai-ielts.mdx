The AI app includes a full **IELTS Speaking** practice mode. Open **IELTS** from the menu.

## Choose a topic

Pick a category to see its mock tests. Each test has **3 parts**, scored independently for **Pronunciation, Fluency, Lexical Resource, and Grammatical Range & Accuracy**.

![IELTS Speaking categories](/images/student-guide/ai-24-ielts.png)

Categories include Society & Culture, Education, Technology & Media, Health & Wellbeing, Environment & Nature, Family & Relationships, and more — each with several mock tests.

## Take a mock test

A test walks you through the IELTS Speaking parts:

- **Part 1** — introduction and interview.
- **Part 2** — the cue-card monologue (speak for up to 2 minutes on a prompt).
- **Part 3** — discussion.

You speak as you would in the real exam, and each part is scored on the four IELTS criteria with a **band score**.

![IELTS test detail with band scores](/images/student-guide/ai-25-ielts-test.png)

## Debrief with the Coach

After a part — or the whole test — use **Talk to Coach again** or **Full mock review with Coach**. The AI coach gives you a spoken debrief: your strengths, your biggest leaks, and exactly what to work on next. You can also **Resume Examiner** to retry.

<Tip>
Use **Part-by-part** debriefs while you're building a skill, and the **full mock review** when you want an exam-realistic run plus an overall plan.
</Tip>
