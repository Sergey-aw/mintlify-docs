## Where to schedule

You can schedule a lesson from three places:

- **Dashboard → Schedule your first lesson**
- **Lessons → Schedule Lesson** (top-right of the lessons page)
- **Students → Schedule** on any student row

![Lessons list showing past and upcoming lessons](/images/teacher-guide/06-lessons-list.png)

The Lessons page is the source of truth for everything: upcoming, completed, cancelled. Filter by student name or email, narrow by date range, or filter by status.

## Schedule lesson or block time

Clicking **Schedule Lesson** opens a dialog with two modes.

![Schedule lesson or block time dialog](/images/teacher-guide/07-schedule-lesson.png)

| Mode | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Schedule Lesson** | Book a 50-minute teaching slot with one of your students. |
| **Block Time Slot** | Block off personal time so students cannot book that window. |

Fields:

- **Student** — pick from your roster.
- **Schedule as free lesson** — toggle on to avoid charging a credit (trial lessons, make-ups).
- **Date** and **Start time** — duration is fixed at 50 minutes; end time is calculated automatically.

Click **Create** to confirm. Both you and the student receive a notification.

## Run the live lesson

When it's time to teach, open the lesson from **Lessons → View → Join lesson**.

![Lesson detail page with Live Lesson and Focus Set](/images/teacher-guide/08-lesson-overview.png)

What you'll see during the lesson:

- A built-in video room — no Zoom or Google Meet needed.
- The student's **Focus Set** (5 words tracked for that lesson) is shown at the top so you can pull them naturally into conversation.
- JustTalk records and transcribes the session in the background to power the post-lesson [AI Lesson Report](/knowledge-base/teacher-guide/lesson-reports).

<Tip>
Encourage students to speak in full sentences. The longer a turn is, the more accurate the AI's vocabulary, grammar, and mistake analysis will be afterwards.
</Tip>
