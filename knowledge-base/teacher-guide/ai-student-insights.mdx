**AI Student Insights** (sidebar, marked *Beta*) is an AI Learning Coach with full context on each student's history. Pick a student, then ask freeform questions.

![AI Student Insights — student picker](/images/teacher-guide/10-ai-insights-list.png)

![AI Student Insights chat with quick action buttons](/images/teacher-guide/11-ai-insights-chat.png)

## What you can ask

- 📊 **Vocabulary and grammar statistics** — *"How many B2 words has Maria used this month?"*
- 🎯 **Learning goals and progress** — *"Is Pedro hitting his fluency goal?"*
- 📚 **Recent lessons and practice** — *"What did we cover last lesson?"*
- 💡 **Weak areas that need attention** — *"What grammar errors keep repeating?"*
- 🔍 **Specific words or grammar structures** — *"Has Anna used the past perfect?"*

## Quick actions

The four buttons at the bottom of the chat launch common questions in one click:

| Button | Asks about |
|---|---|
| **My Progress** | Overall trajectory across recent lessons. |
| **Vocabulary Stats** | CEFR distribution and growth. |
| **Suggest Goals** | Recommended next learning goals based on patterns. |
| **Weak Areas** | Recurring mistake categories. |

## Past conversations

Past conversations are saved per student in the right panel — revisit them before the next lesson to refresh your memory.

<Tip>
A good Friday habit: open AI Student Insights for each student you saw that week and ask *"What should we work on next lesson?"*. Paste the answer into [Messages](/knowledge-base/teacher-guide/messaging) as a heads-up.
</Tip>
