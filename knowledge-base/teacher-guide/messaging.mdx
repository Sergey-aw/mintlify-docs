**Messages** in the sidebar is your direct-message inbox with every student.

![Messages inbox](/images/teacher-guide/14-messages.png)

## What's in the inbox

- **Student threads** — one per student you've messaged or who has messaged you.
- **System messages** — e.g. *Lesson Scheduled* notifications from JustTalk itself appear in the same inbox.
- The unread badge on the sidebar reflects all unread threads.

## Starting a conversation

Two ways:

- Click **Start conversation** on the Messages page to pick from your roster.
- Click **Message** on any row of the [Students](/knowledge-base/teacher-guide/students) roster.

## What to send

- **Post-lesson summaries** — paste the output of **Generate Summary** from the [lesson report](/knowledge-base/teacher-guide/lesson-reports).
- **Homework** — reading links, vocabulary lists, audio notes.
- **Schedule changes** — confirm rescheduling or cancellations in writing.
- **Encouragement** — a one-line "great job today" goes a long way for student retention.

<Tip>
Keep all coaching context in JustTalk Messages rather than scattering across WhatsApp / email. Your future self (and any backup teacher) will thank you.
</Tip>
