Welcome to JustTalk. This guide walks you through every step a teacher needs to take — from setting up an account to running lessons and reviewing AI-generated insights. Read the pages in order on your first day; come back to individual pages as a reference later.

## What you can do on JustTalk

| Area | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Quick-start checklist, upcoming lessons, and onboarding tips. |
| **Lessons** | Schedule, join, and review every live lesson. |
| **Calendar** | Manage your weekly availability so students can self-book. |
| **Students** | Invite students by email and see each one's lesson history. |
| **AI Student Insights** | Chat with an AI that has read all of a student's lessons. |
| **JustTalk AI** | Build custom AI agents and workflows for students to practice with. |
| **Messages** | Direct-message students and receive system notifications. |

## Quick-start checklist

Your dashboard surfaces four quick-start steps. Complete all four before booking your first paid lesson.

![Teacher dashboard with the Get Started checklist](/images/teacher-guide/01-dashboard.png)

1. **Set your hourly rate** — what students are charged per lesson.
2. **Set up your availability** — when students can book you.
3. **Invite your students** — bring your existing students onto JustTalk.
4. **Schedule your first lesson** — confirm a time with a student.

Each card on the dashboard links straight to the right screen. The progress badge in the top-right shows how many steps you've completed.

<Tip>
Stuck? The dashboard has **Help docs** and **Ask support** links under the checklist.
</Tip>
