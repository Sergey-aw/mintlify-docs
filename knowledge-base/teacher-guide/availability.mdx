Open **Calendar** in the sidebar to see your week. Use the **Week / Month** toggle at the top right and the arrows to navigate between weeks.

![Calendar week view](/images/teacher-guide/02-calendar.png)

## Manage weekly availability

Click **Manage Availability** to open the drag-to-create editor.

![Manage Weekly Availability editor](/images/teacher-guide/03-manage-availability.png)

- **Drag** on an empty cell to create an availability slot.
- **Drag a slot** to move it to a different time or day.
- **Drag the bottom edge** of a slot to resize it.
- Times use the time zone shown at the top of the editor — confirm it matches yours before saving.
- Click **Save Availability** when you're done. Open slots become bookable immediately.

<Note>
Marking a slot available does not auto-confirm a booking — students still send a booking request that fills the slot, and you can decline if a conflict comes up.
</Note>

## Tips

- **Keep it fresh.** An empty or stale calendar means no bookings.
- **Block time you can't teach.** Use **Lessons → Schedule Lesson → Block Time Slot** to reserve windows for personal time.
- **Time zones travel.** Update the editor's time zone whenever you change locations so students don't see a stale schedule.
